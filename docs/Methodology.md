# Methodology

## Project
**Child Fever & Care-Seeking Power BI Dashboard**

## Analytical Approach

This project uses a descriptive analytical approach to examine healthcare-seeking behavior among caregivers of children experiencing fever or acute febrile illness (AFI).

The analysis focuses primarily on the timing of care-seeking and explores how it varies across caregiver characteristics, healthcare access, knowledge, perceptions, and healthcare pathways.

## Primary Analytical Outcome

The main analytical outcome is **Care-Seeking Time**, categorized into:

- **Within 24 Hours** — timely care-seeking
- **After 24 Hours** — delayed care-seeking

## Key Analytical Dimensions

### 1. Caregiver Characteristics
- Age
- Gender
- Educational level
- Occupation
- Household income
- Healthcare decision-making

### 2. Knowledge and Perceptions
- Fever definition
- Perceived seriousness of fever
- Knowledge of danger signs
- Expected response to fever

### 3. Healthcare Access
- Physical accessibility
- Service affordability
- Transportation/access-related factors
- Healthcare facility type

### 4. Care-Seeking Pathway
- First action taken
- Facility visited
- Referral
- Hospitalization
- Clinical outcome

## Power BI Workflow

1. Data preparation and cleaning
2. Data validation
3. Data modeling
4. Creation of DAX measures
5. Development of interactive visuals
6. Cross-filtering and slicer configuration
7. Validation of dashboard results
8. Interpretation of descriptive patterns

## Dashboard Structure

1. **Executive Overview & Timeliness**
2. **Healthcare Access & Barriers**
3. **Knowledge, Perception & Danger Signs**
4. **Care-Seeking Pathways & Outcomes**

## Analytical Interpretation

The dashboard presents descriptive comparisons between timely and delayed care-seeking groups.

These comparisons should not be interpreted as evidence that a characteristic directly causes delayed care-seeking.
